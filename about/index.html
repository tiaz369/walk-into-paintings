走进名画是一座可以走进去的线上美术馆。每一幅名画都被做成一个可以探索的空间：走进画里，发现细节，听它背后的故事，最后去博物馆看真迹。

Walk into Paintings is an online gallery you can step inside. Each painting becomes a space to explore: walk in, discover details, hear the story behind it, then go and see the real thing in a museum.

它不想替代原画，而是想让你更想去看原画。

It is not meant to replace the original, but to make you want to see it.

关于我：我是 Tia，住在伦敦，学过电影和教育。我不是程序员。网站的代码由 AI（Anthropic 的 Claude）编写；创意、故事、文字和每一条事实，都由我撰写和核对。

About me: I'm Tia, based in London, with a background in film studies and education. I'm not a programmer. The code is written by AI (Claude, by Anthropic); the concept, stories, texts and every fact are written and checked by me.

内容原则：只使用已进入公有领域的画作；每条事实都有可靠来源；解读性的内容会标明是解读；图片来源注明在每个场景中。

Content principles: only public-domain paintings; every fact has a reliable source; interpretations are marked as such; image sources are credited in each scene.
