向日葵 Sunflowers

向日葵 / Sunflowers · 梵高 / Vincent van Gogh · 1888年8月 / August 1888 · 伦敦国家美术馆 / The National Gallery, London

图片：Wikimedia Commons，公有领域 / Image: Wikimedia Commons, public domain

发现 · Discover

黄上加黄 Yellow on yellow

黄色的花、黄色的桌面、黄色的背景。他计划用这些画装饰房间，让整体成为"一首蓝与黄的交响曲"。

Yellow flowers, yellow table, yellow background. He planned these paintings to decorate a room as "a symphony in blue and yellow".

花瓶上的签名 The signature

他在花瓶上签了名："Vincent"。

He signed his name on the vase: "Vincent".

从花苞到凋谢 From bud to wilting

十五朵向日葵，有的含苞，有的盛开，有的已经凋谢。

Fifteen sunflowers: some in bud, some in full bloom, some already wilting.

给高更的房间 For Gauguin's room

这幅画是为黄房子的客房准备的，用来迎接高更。

This painting was made for the guest room of the Yellow House, to welcome Gauguin.

厚厚的颜料 Thick paint

颜料涂得很厚，可以看到笔触的起伏。

The paint is laid on thickly; you can see the ridges of each brushstroke.

高更的称赞 Gauguin's praise

高更很喜欢这幅画，称赞它完美体现了梵高的风格。

Gauguin admired it, praising it as a perfect example of Van Gogh's style.

高更画梵高 Gauguin's portrait

和梵高同住时，高更画了一幅《画向日葵的梵高》，现藏阿姆斯特丹梵高博物馆。

While living with Van Gogh, Gauguin painted "Van Gogh Painting Sunflowers", now in the Van Gogh Museum, Amsterdam.

故事 · The story

等待高更 Waiting for Gauguin

1888年8月，梵高画了一组向日葵，用来装饰黄房子的客房，迎接高更。

In August 1888, Van Gogh painted a group of sunflowers to decorate the Yellow House guest room for Gauguin.

1888年8月 · 伦敦国家美术馆 / August 1888 · The National Gallery, London

一场争执 A quarrel

高更离开阿尔勒后，想要一幅向日葵来换他留下的习作，梵高很不高兴。

After leaving Arles, Gauguin asked for one of the sunflower paintings in exchange for studies he had left behind. Van Gogh was upset.

来到伦敦 To London

1924年，伦敦国家美术馆从梵高家人手中买下了这幅画。

In 1924, the National Gallery in London bought this painting from Van Gogh's family.

向日葵，在某种意义上属于我。 The sunflower is mine, in a way.

关于画家 · About the artist

文森特·梵高（1853–1890），荷兰画家。他将近27岁才开始画画，短短十年留下了大量作品。1888年他来到法国南部的阿尔勒，在那里画出了许多最著名的画。

Vincent van Gogh (1853–1890) was a Dutch painter. He began painting at around 27 and left a huge body of work in just ten years. In 1888 he moved to Arles in the south of France, where he painted many of his best-known works.
