常见问题 · Questions

我的照片会怎么处理？ · What happens to my photos?

只用来制作你的作品。照片里的人会被裁掉，交付后 30 天内删除原图。照片不会放到网站上。

They are used only to make your piece. Any people in them are cropped out, and the originals are deleted within 30 days of delivery. Photos are never put on the website.
