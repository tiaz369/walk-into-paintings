一位画家，也是一位收藏家 The Painter Who Collected

卡耶博特画画，也买下朋友们的画。 / Caillebotte painted, and bought his friends' paintings too.

古斯塔夫·卡耶博特出身富裕家庭。他是印象派画家，也是他们的朋友和支持者。他买下了许多朋友们的画，去世后，这些画进入了法国国家收藏。 / Gustave Caillebotte came from a wealthy family. He was an Impressionist painter, and a friend and supporter of the others. He bought many of his friends' paintings, and after his death they entered the French national collection.

  1. 刨地板的工人 · The Floor Scrapers — 1875年，官方沙龙拒绝了他的这幅画。第二年，他在印象派画展上首次参展。 / In 1875, the official Salon rejected this painting. The following year, he made his debut at the Impressionist exhibition.
  2. 巴黎街道，雨天 · Paris Street; Rainy Day — 1877年的印象派画展，很大程度上是他自己组织的。他在那里展出了这幅雨中的巴黎。 / The Impressionist exhibition of 1877 was largely organised by Caillebotte himself. He showed this rainy Paris there.
  3. 圣拉扎尔火车站 · The Saint-Lazare Station — 同一场画展上，莫奈展出了他的火车站。这一幅后来属于卡耶博特。 / At the same exhibition, Monet showed his paintings of the station. This one later belonged to Caillebotte.
  4. 煎饼磨坊的舞会 · Dance at Le Moulin de la Galette — 1879年到1894年，雷诺阿的这幅画也属于他。 / From 1879 to 1894, this painting by Renoir belonged to him too.
  5. 芭蕾（明星） · Ballet (The Star) — 德加的这幅芭蕾，同样是他的收藏。 / This ballet scene by Degas was also in his collection.
  6. 游艇上的午宴 · Luncheon of the Boating Party — 在雷诺阿的这幅画里，卡耶博特自己也在：右下角反坐在椅子上的，就是他。 / In this painting by Renoir, Caillebotte appears himself: he is the man sitting backwards on his chair, lower right.

1894年卡耶博特去世后，他的收藏进入了法国国家收藏。今天，其中很多画都挂在奥赛博物馆。 / After Caillebotte's death in 1894, his collection entered the French national collection. Today many of these paintings hang in the Musée d'Orsay.
