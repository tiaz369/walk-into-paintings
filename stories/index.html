艺术故事 Stories

用几幅画，串起一段故事。 / A few paintings, one story.

  • 阿尔勒的一天 · A Day in Arles — 一条想象的路线，串起梵高1888年画下的地方。 / An imagined route through the places Van Gogh painted in 1888.
  • 一位画家，也是一位收藏家 · The Painter Who Collected — 卡耶博特画画，也买下朋友们的画。 / Caillebotte painted, and bought his friends' paintings too.
  • 德加的芭蕾 · Degas at the Ballet — 从教室、练功房、后台，到舞台。 / From the classroom and the practice room to the wings and the stage.
  • 被拒绝的画 · Rejected — 它们都曾被官方沙龙拒绝，今天却挂在世界上最重要的博物馆里。 / All of them were once rejected by the official Salon. Today they hang in some of the world's great museums.
